# Immagine hero — prompt di generazione

L'immagine hero di [GPForum](https://github.com/gpicchiarelli/GPForum) è un render
fotorealistico cinematografico (1672 × 941, formato 16:9): stanza in penombra, luce dorata
radente, materiali veri (legno, pietra, metallo), un elemento a parete che racconta il software
e, sullo sfondo, un angolo tecnologico con rack e un monitor di metriche.

Questo prompt applica lo stesso linguaggio a ArcDocDB: **villa di campagna, legno e ottone,
tecnologia discreta**, con le stanze come concetti del progetto (l'Archivio dei segmenti
immutabili, i Registri, la bottega della compaction, il locale tecnico).

**File atteso:** `assets/img/arcdocdb-hero.png` (16:9, almeno 1672 × 941). Quando c'è, va
aggiunto in cima al `README.md` come in GPForum, con il testo alternativo qui sotto.

## Prompt (inglese, per generatori di immagini)

```
Photorealistic cinematic interior, 16:9, shot like an architectural photograph at golden hour.
A quiet, dimly lit study inside a country villa, warm oak and walnut timber everywhere:
vertical slatted wood panelling on the left, exposed dark beams on the ceiling with small brass
track spotlights, a wide-plank oak floor with soft reflections and long amber light shafts
falling across it from tall steel-framed windows on the right that open onto a green Tuscan
hillside with cypress trees in soft focus.

The main wall is a monumental wall-mounted archive of identical leather-bound ledgers on dark
oak shelves, hundreds of volumes in tidy rows, every spine with a small brass label. One single
open ledger rests on a lit oak lectern in front of it, a fountain pen beside it, a subtle warm
glow on its pages. A slim concrete-and-brass strip light runs along the base of the shelves.

In the left background, through a glass-and-black-steel door, a small technical room: two
server racks with softly glowing green and amber status LEDs and a wall monitor showing a calm
latency graph. The technology is discreet, integrated into the wood, never dominant.

Foreground right: a long walnut table with a brass reading lamp, a ceramic cup, a closed
laptop, a small brass hourglass and a few paper index cards. A mid-century chair in the lower
left foreground, slightly out of focus. Large potted green plants near the panelling and by
the window.

Mood: calm, serious, durable, trustworthy, "built to be operated for decades". Warm amber
highlights against deep green-brown shadows, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, volumetric
dust in the light, high dynamic range, 35mm lens, ultra detailed, realistic materials.
No people, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Prompt negativo (dove supportato)

```
cartoon, illustration, flat vector, 3d render look, plastic, oversaturated, neon colors,
cyberpunk, futuristic, sci-fi, people, faces, text, letters, logos, watermark, clutter
```

## Variante «stanze tematiche» (se si vuole più evidenza dei concetti)

Aggiungere al prompt: *«a long hallway of the villa with three open arched doorways in the
background, each showing a different room in the same timber-and-brass style: a registry room
with a wall of small oak catalogue drawers and a unrolled paper scroll on a desk; a workshop with
a workbench where a large worn ledger stands next to a small crisp new one and a brass
hourglass; a quiet room with a single writing desk and a lamp.»*

## Testo alternativo per il README

> A quiet country-villa archive in warm timber and brass: a wall of identical ledgers, a single
> open ledger under a lamp, and a discreet server room glowing in the background.
